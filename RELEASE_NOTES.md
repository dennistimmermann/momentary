First release.

- Tap a modifier on its own to send a key; hold it or use it with another key and it stays a modifier
- One rule per modifier, left and right told apart — ships with both ⌘ keys → Escape, everything else off
- Record any combination as the output, not just Escape
- Holding a modifier lights up its row, so you can tell which one a remapped key reports as
- Adjustable hold threshold
- Stands down while a listed app is frontmost
- No Dock icon, one menu bar item, and the settings drop out of it
- Turning the menu bar item off leaves nothing on screen at all; the settings become a window
- Launching Momentary while it is already running opens its settings either way
